# 末班渡輪 1949 Last Ferry Rush 1949

類型 Genre: 偽 3D 賽車 Pseudo-3D racer (OutRun / Chase H.Q. style) · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `index.html`

## 中文

1948–49年，你開著一輛徵用來的國軍卡車，車斗載滿十二位乘客，趕搭最後一班撤退渡輪。政府說這只是暫時撤退。六段路，從起火的村莊一路開到碼頭；路障、橋梁，還有越來越大片的地圖，都在共軍手上。車資以金圓券計價：跳錶很準，錢不准。

**六段路**（每段之前都有一頁像素劇情，結束後有本段結算）

1. **村口小路**（1948年11月）：起火的村莊、牛車、難民和一頭中立的驢子。關底：從後面撞翻裝甲車「原本是我們的」，閃開它丟下來的油桶和砲彈。
2. **雪中山口**（1949年1月）：路面結冰，卡車會滑（冬季胎春天才發）。路障已經「和平改編」，撞上去會少兩位乘客。關底：宣傳車「好消息」，邊開邊廣播「投降吧！我們有米！」，還丟傳單；撞上傳單整個畫面都是紙。
3. **霧中田埂**（1949年3月）：田埂窄得只容一輛卡車，兩旁是爛泥，又起大霧，水牛擋路。關底：後照鏡裡的戰車「二手車主」從霧中開砲，紅色記號是落點，快閃開。
4. **最後一座橋**（1949年4月）：工兵按時計酬，所以提早開炸。關底：大橋在你身後一段一段炸掉，後照鏡裡的爆炸越來越近，橋面還有破洞。
5. **上海街頭**（1949年5月）：雨夜外灘，黃包車、電車、擠兌人潮（按喇叭也不會讓），天上飄著金圓券。關底：撞停電車「下一站：解放」，它只能在兩條軌道之間換來換去。
6. **碼頭**（1949年12月）：棧橋兩旁都是海，貨箱擋路。最終關底：裝甲車「原本是我們的 二號」，撞翻之後衝向碼頭盡頭的渡輪。

**特殊機制**

- 時間：通過檢查站（還是我們的）加時間；進入關底前再補一次時間。
- 乘客：撞車會掉乘客（撞路障、電車、中砲掉兩位），撞到難民或擠兌人潮只會急煞減速。路邊招手的人開過去就能載上車。乘客全跑光或時間用完就要接關。
- 喇叭：把牛車、黃包車、腳踏車和難民趕到路邊。驢子和水牛保持中立，不理你。
- 車資一直以金圓券累積，數字越來越大；畫面上同時顯示能換幾粒米，而米價漲得比車資還快。
- 接關次數無限（付金圓券），也可以重跑該段。進度自動存檔，可從路線日誌選擇已解鎖的路段。

**結局**：你趕上了最後一班渡輪，卡車也一起上船，旁邊還有兩架鋼琴、一個檔案櫃，和某位長官的金魚。與此同時，總部作戰地圖整片變紅（戰略轉進）。船開到一半事務長收船錢，你一路收的金圓券剛好買一杯茶，杯子不算；國庫的黃金早就坐頭等艙先過去了。到了台灣（暫時），乘客問：「我們什麼時候回去？」軍官說：「明年。」他每一年都說得非常肯定；過了幾十年，大家吃年夜飯時只是互相說一句「明年」。最後是片尾名單和統計：路段 6／6，回程票明年有效（每年都是），打贏的內戰 0／1。劇終（暫時）。

**操作**

- 鍵盤：↑／W 油門，↓／S 煞車，←→／A D 轉彎，J 或空白鍵按喇叭；P 暫停，L 切換語言，M 音效；Enter 翻頁／開始，Esc 跳過結局頁。路線日誌按 1–6 選段。
- 手機：橫向遊玩。卡車自動前進，左下 ◀ ▶ 轉彎，右下「煞車」減速、「喇叭」趕路人；劇情與結算頁點一下畫面繼續。右上角可切換語言（EN／中文）、暫停、開關音效。直向時會提示把手機轉橫。

## English

1948–49. You drive a requisitioned Nationalist (KMT) army truck with twelve passengers in the back to the last evacuation ferry. The government is relocating. Temporarily, it says. Six legs, from a burning village to the docks; the Communists hold the roadblocks, the bridges and, increasingly, the map. Fares are paid in Gold Yuan: the meter is accurate, the money is not.

**The six legs** (each opens with a pixel story page and ends with a results screen)

1. **The Village Road** (Nov 1948): burning houses, carts, refugees and a neutral donkey. Set piece: ram the armored car "Formerly Ours" from behind while dodging the barrels and shells it throws back.
2. **The Snow Pass** (Jan 1949): ice, so the truck slides (winter tyres will be issued in spring). The roadblocks have "reorganized peacefully" and cost two passengers each. Set piece: the loudspeaker truck "Good News", broadcasting "SURRENDER! WE HAVE RICE!" and dropping leaflet bundles that cover your windscreen in paper.
3. **The Paddy Dykes** (Mar 1949): a dyke one truck wide, mud on both sides, thick fog, a water buffalo. Set piece: the tank "Second Owner" shells you from the fog behind (shown in a rear-view mirror); red marks on the road show where shells will land.
4. **The Last Bridge** (Apr 1949): the engineers are paid by the hour, so they start early. Set piece: outrun the demolition as the bridge is blown up section by section behind you, with holes opening in the deck.
5. **Shanghai Streets** (May 1949): the Bund at night in the rain, rickshaws, trams, bank-run crowds that do not move for horns, Gold Yuan blowing in the wind. Set piece: stop the tram "Next Stop: Liberation", which can only switch between its two tracks.
6. **The Docks** (Dec 1949): a pier with water on both sides and crates in the way. Final set piece: the armored car "Formerly Ours II", then the run to the ferry at the end of the pier.

**Special mechanics**

- Time: checkpoints (still ours) add time; more is added when a set piece starts.
- Passengers: crashes cost passengers (roadblocks, trams and shell hits cost two); refugees and bank queues only make you swerve and slow down. Drive through hitchhikers waving at the roadside to pick them up. Run out of passengers or time and you need a continue.
- Horn: moves carts, rickshaws, bicycles and refugees to the side. The donkey and the buffalo are neutral and ignore it.
- The fare meter counts Gold Yuan and the number keeps growing; next to it is what it buys in grains of rice, and rice rises faster than your fares.
- Unlimited continues (paid in Gold Yuan), or restart the leg. Progress saves automatically; pick any unlocked leg from the road log.

**Ending**: you make the last ferry, truck included, along with two pianos, a filing cabinet and an officer's goldfish. Meanwhile, HQ's situation map turns red ("strategic transfer"). Halfway across, the purser collects the fares: everything you earned in Gold Yuan buys one cup of tea, not the cup; the gold reserves crossed months ago in first class. In Taiwan (temporarily), the passengers ask "When do we go back?" "Next year," says the officer, every year; after a few decades they just say it to each other at New Year's dinner. Credits and a tally close it out: legs 6/6, return tickets valid next year (every year), civil wars won 0/1. The End (temporarily).

**Controls**

- Keyboard: ↑/W gas, ↓/S brake, ←→/A D steer, J or Space horn; P pause, L language, M sound; Enter to advance pages and start, Esc skips ending pages. Press 1–6 in the road log to pick a leg.
- Phone: play in landscape. The truck accelerates on its own; ◀ ▶ bottom left steer, BRAKE and HORN bottom right; tap the screen to advance story and results pages. Top-right buttons switch language (EN / 中文), pause and toggle sound. In portrait a hint asks you to turn the phone sideways.
