# 黃金大轉進 1949 Gold Run 1949

類型 Genre: 塔防 Tower defense · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `index.html`

## 中文

上海，1948–49年。國府的黃金儲備正趁著夜色悄悄運往台灣，官方說法是「什麼事也沒發生」。全城的人都想留點紀念品：趁火打劫的、擠兌的市民、投機客、逃兵、軍閥，最後還有共軍。你是護金部隊（國軍），從銀行金庫一路守到最後一艘船。每張地圖有 20 箱黃金，被摸走一箱少一箱。至於戰爭打得怎樣，那是別的部門的事。

**六張地圖**（每張之前都有一頁像素劇情，最後一波都是魔王）

1. **銀行金庫**（1948年12月）：大理石柱和辦公桌之間的紅地毯。敵人：趁火打劫的、銀行職員、擠兌人潮、逃兵。魔王：**內鬼**，扛著一袋金圓券從正門走進來。
2. **外灘**（1948年12月）：江邊的夜路，金圓券崩盤，擠兌潮成群衝來，還有投機客和會讓身邊敵人跑更快的軍閥。解鎖**印鈔機**。魔王：**黑市卡車**。
3. **鐵路貨場**（1949年1月）：徐蚌會戰後的北站，運金列車在鐵軌上跑。共軍幹部、手推車隊、騎兵登場。解鎖**迫擊砲**。魔王：**裝甲軌道車**。
4. **江邊碼頭**（1949年4月）：長江「天險」失守，敵人從兩個入口上岸，民兵和工兵穿著護甲。解鎖**擴音器**。魔王：**陣前起義的將軍**，騎白馬，帽子顏色一直換來換去。
5. **機場**（1949年5月）：最後幾班飛機載走官員、檔案、家具和一架鋼琴。魔王：**戰車（前任車主六位）**。
6. **最後一艘船**（最終關）：兩條路在碼頭會合，所有敵人一起上。守到船開。魔王：**同一輛戰車（車主第七位）**，插著新旗子。

**防禦單位與機制**

- **步槍班**：可靠，每月發餉（理論上）。**機槍巢**：射得快、射程短，彈藥是美援，帳單還沒付。**迫擊砲**：範圍傷害，太近打不到，跟政策一樣。**擴音器**：讓敵人減速，有些人會當場投誠，轉身去打自己人。**印鈔機**：每波開始時印錢。
- **通貨膨脹就是規則**：每一波物價漲 8%，每台印鈔機再多漲 3%。升級和建造越來越貴，錢越印越多，也越來越不值錢。
- 每個單位可升兩級；賣掉退一半。重裝敵人有護甲，小口徑子彈打起來比較痛苦。
- 每張地圖三顆星：保住 20 箱三星，14 箱以上兩星。進度、最佳紀錄、語言和音效設定自動存檔，可從地圖選單重打任何已解鎖的關卡。

**結局**：你把黃金守得完美無缺。黃金趁夜開往台灣，收據上寫著「暫時遷移」，非常暫時，到現在還是這樣寫。上海換了主人，金圓券終於回到真正的價值：零，店家拿來糊牆。黃金先走了，十二月政府跟著撤到台灣；黃金分到頭等艙，你搭下一班船，在船上領到一整疊金圓券欠餉，拿來搧風，這是它最好的用途。首都、黃金和你都「暫時」安頓下來，每年元旦的命令都一樣：明年就反攻大陸。月曆一路翻過去。最後是片尾名單（理財顧問：一隻海鷗；中立觀察員：一頭驢子；回程：明年）和戰績統計：守住地圖 6／6，打贏的內戰 0／1，劇終（暫時）。

**操作**

- 滑鼠：點上方列的單位，再點空地建造（游標會顯示射程）；點已建好的單位可升級或賣掉。右上角是「下一波」、加速、暫停（暫停選單裡可切換語言）和音效。
- 鍵盤：1–5 選單位，空白鍵 下一波，F 加速，P 或 Esc 暫停，M 靜音，L 切換語言，Esc 取消選取；劇情頁按 Enter 或空白鍵繼續，地圖選單按 1–6 選關。
- 手機：橫向遊玩。點上方單位，再點同一塊空地兩下（第一下預覽位置和射程，第二下確認建造）；點已建好的單位會跳出「升級／賣掉」大按鈕。劇情頁點任何地方繼續，右上角可切換 EN／中文。直拿手機會提示轉橫。

## English

Shanghai, 1948–49. The Nationalist gold reserves are leaving for Taiwan, quietly, by night; officially, nothing is happening. Everyone in the city would like a souvenir: looters, bank-run crowds, speculators, deserters, warlords, and eventually the Communist army. You are the KMT gold garrison. Guard the crates from the bank vault to the last ship. Each map has 20 crates, and every one that walks off is gone. How the war goes is somebody else's department.

**The six maps** (each opens with a pixel-art story page and ends with a boss wave)

1. **The Bank Vault** (December 1948): a red carpet between marble pillars and desks. Looters, bank clerks, bank-run crowds and deserters. Boss: **The Inside Man**, walking out the front door with a sack of Gold Yuan.
2. **The Bund** (December 1948): the riverfront at night as the Gold Yuan collapses; crowds rush in packs, with speculators and warlords who make everyone near them hurry. Unlocks the **Printing Press**. Boss: **the Black-Market Truck**.
3. **The Rail Yard** (January 1949): North Station after Huaihai, with the gold train running past. Cadres, handcarts and cavalry arrive. Unlocks the **Mortar**. Boss: **the Armored Handcar**.
4. **The River Docks** (April 1949): the "impassable" Yangtze has been passed; enemies land from two entrances, and militia and sappers wear armor. Unlocks the **Loudspeaker**. Boss: **the General Who Switched Sides**, on a white horse, whose cap keeps changing colour.
5. **The Airfield** (May 1949): the last planes out carry officials, files, furniture and a piano. Boss: **the Tank (six previous owners)**.
6. **The Last Ship** (final map): two roads merge on the pier and everyone comes at once. Hold until the ship sails. Boss: **the same tank, now with seven owners** and a new flag.

**Units and mechanics**

- **Rifle Squad**: reliable, paid monthly, in theory. **MG Nest**: fast, short range; the ammo is American, invoice pending. **Mortar**: splash damage, cannot hit anything close, like policy. **Loudspeaker**: slows enemies, and some defect on the spot and turn on their friends. **Printing Press**: prints money at the start of every wave.
- **Inflation is a rule**: prices rise 8% every wave, plus 3% for each printing press you own. Building and upgrading get more expensive; you print more money, and it buys less.
- Every unit upgrades twice; selling refunds half. Armored enemies shrug off small-calibre hits.
- Three stars per map: 20 crates saved for three, 14 or more for two. Progress, best results, language and sound are saved automatically; any unlocked map can be replayed from the map select.

**Ending**: you guarded the gold perfectly. It sails for Taiwan by night with a receipt that says TEMPORARY RELOCATION. Very temporary. It still says that. Shanghai changes hands and the Gold Yuan finally reaches its true value: zero, cheaper than wallpaper. The gold left first; in December the government followed it to Taiwan. The gold got a first-class cabin; you followed on a later boat, where your back pay arrives in Gold Yuan and makes an excellent fan. The capital, the gold and you all settle in "temporarily", and every New Year the order is the same: we counterattack the mainland next year, while the calendar keeps flipping. Credits roll (financial advice: a seagull; neutral observer: a donkey; return trip: next year), then the tally: maps held 6/6, civil wars won 0/1, THE END (TEMPORARILY).

**Controls**

- Mouse: click a unit in the top bar, then an empty tile to build (the cursor shows its range); click a built unit to upgrade or sell it. The top-right buttons are Next Wave, fast forward, pause (the pause menu has the language switch) and sound.
- Keyboard: 1–5 choose a unit, Space next wave, F fast forward, P or Esc pause, M mute, L switch language, Esc cancel a selection; Enter or Space advances story pages; 1–6 pick a map on the map select.
- Phone: play in landscape. Tap a unit in the top bar, then tap the same empty tile twice (the first tap previews the spot and range, the second builds); tap a built unit for big Upgrade / Sell buttons. Tap anywhere to advance story pages; the top-right button switches EN / 中文. Holding the phone upright shows a "turn sideways" hint.
