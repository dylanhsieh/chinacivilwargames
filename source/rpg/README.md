# 明暗：第四十八遠征隊 Ming An: Expedition 48

類型 Genre: 3D 探索＋回合制 RPG（即時閃避／格擋）3D exploration + turn-based RPG with real-time dodge and parry (in the spirit of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33) · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `index.html`

## 中文

1948年，上海。印鈔機每年都在鈔票上畫一個更大的數字，積蓄比那個數字還不值錢的人，就會被抹除。局勢「完全在掌控之中」的國民政府，派出第四十八遠征隊去阻止它；前面四十七隊後來怎麼了，沒人記得。第四十七隊只留下一張紙條：多帶點錢。你帶領三人小隊（全是國軍這一邊），從外灘一路走到最後的碼頭，在 3D 街道上探索、撿道具、在茶爐邊休息，碰到敵人就進入回合制戰鬥。

**遠征隊**

- **老王**：被抓來的壯丁，揹大刀。大刀劈砍、幹譙怒吼（全隊傷害提升）、債務旋風、背水一戰、欠餉連斬（每欠一個月餉砍一刀）。
- **小藍**：宣傳員，拿大聲公。捷報轟炸、戰地包紮、忠誠調查表（四十頁）、新生活運動（全隊復活回血，衣服紮好）、勝利新聞片（1938年拍的）。
- **會計老錢**：記帳員，揹步槍。瞄準射擊、查帳、開動印鈔、惡性通膨（累積「通膨」層數一次花光）、幣制改革（劃掉三個零）。

**六章**（每章之間都有一頁像素劇情：日期、地點、史實一句、笑話一句）

1. **上海外灘**：黑市黃牛、扒手、扛門板的警察、囤米商，還有一個餓了的補給箱。頭目：**囤積大王**（黑市生意，白手套；會收買警察）。
2. **繩村**：被解放兩次、被抓壯丁三次的村子。空心壯丁、草叉民兵、步槍兵、地下煽動員、擲彈兵。頭目：**魏政委**（自我批評督導員；批鬥大會、整風）。
3. **沒人炸的那座橋**：炸橋預算用金圓券付，只買到半根炸藥。憲兵、步槍兵、擲彈兵。頭目：**鐵將軍**（把守上鎖的橋；半血後大刀著火）。
4. **不打烊的印鈔廠**：印一張鈔票的成本比面額還高。印鈔廠職員、會走路的鈔票堆，還有一座餓了的銀行金庫。頭目：**趙司庫**（凍結帳戶扣 AP，半血後「緊急發行」）。
5. **淮海**：雪地戰壕，五十萬大軍往另一邊走了。人海戰術、迫擊砲班、步槍兵。頭目：**盧上校**（忠誠度可以商量；半血時陣前起義，帽子變綠，刀還是同一把）。
6. **末班渡口**：雨夜碼頭。海關稽查員、碼頭苦力、憲兵。最終頭目：**印鈔機**（在隊員身上畫「48」，四回合後抹除，閃避或格擋可以把數字弄糊；半血後惡性通膨）。

**成長與道具**：戰鬥得經驗升級（生命、攻擊上升，等級 2–8 學新技能）；地圖上有米酒、萬金油、美援罐頭和 8 個護身符（配給券、美援手錶、炒菜鍋、國民黨黨證、會計的算盤、M1 鋼盔，兩個寶箱怪身上還有幸運金圓券和勝利公債）。每章頭目掉落章節信物。茶爐可以休息回滿、向軍需官買道具、在點燃過的茶爐之間傳送；但每休息一次物價漲 15%，小怪也會重生。每個茶爐和每場戰鬥後自動存檔，可從標題「繼續遠征」。

**戰鬥機制**

- 依速度排出行動順序（畫面左上）。攻擊 +1 AP；技能花 AP；射擊花 1 AP，自由瞄準準星，打中發光的錢包就是弱點（大傷害）；道具不花 AP。
- 技能出招時會出現收縮的圓圈：白圈縮到金圈時按下，打出「不錯」或「完美」加成。
- 敵人回合：攻擊命中的瞬間按閃避（時機較寬）或格擋（時機較緊，+1 AP）。一招的每一下都格擋住，就會自動反擊。
- 每個敵人有架勢條，打滿就「破防」：跳過一回合、受到傷害 +50%。印鈔機破防時會清掉所有「48」。
- 探索時先砍敵人一刀可以偷襲（敵人少血、我方先動）。全隊倒下＝「轉進」：回到最後的茶爐，金圓券貶值一半。

**結局**：遠征隊打倒了印鈔機，數字不再變大……然後前線準時抵達。大家還是上了船：黃金儲備好幾個月前就走了（頭等艙、木箱標著「文件」），船票不收金圓券，但金圓券可以壓艙。1949年12月，國民政府撤退到台灣，官方說法是「暫時轉進」，非常暫時，老王連行李都沒拆。「明年就反攻大陸！」每一年都這麼說，日曆一直翻。老錢把最後一張金圓券裱框，這是唯一從沒貶值的投資，因為它一開始就是零。接著是片尾名單（中立觀察員：一頭驢子；理財顧問：一隻海鷗）和戰績：章節 6／6、頭目 6／6、打贏的內戰 0／1，劇終（暫時）。

**操作**

- 鍵盤＋滑鼠：W A S D 移動，點一下畫面鎖定滑鼠轉視角（方向鍵也可以），Shift 跑步，E 使用（茶爐、道具、地上留言、迷霧），J 或滑鼠左鍵先出手偷襲。戰鬥：W S（或方向鍵）選擇，J／Enter 確認，K／Esc 返回，A D 切換目標，技能圓圈和射擊時按 J；敵人回合空白鍵閃避、Shift 格擋。P 暫停，L 切換語言，M 音效。戰鬥選單也可以直接用滑鼠點。
- 手機：橫向遊玩（直向會提示轉橫）。左半邊是虛擬搖桿，右半邊拖曳轉視角，「出手」偷襲、「跑」、「使用」按鈕；戰鬥中直接點選單和目標，技能圓圈時點畫面任何地方，敵人回合按「閃避」／「格擋」大按鈕。右上角可切換語言（EN／中文）、暫停、開關音效。

## English

Shanghai, 1948. Every year the Printer paints a bigger number on the banknote, and everyone whose savings are worth less than the number is erased. The Nationalist government, firmly in control, sends Expedition 48 to stop it; nobody remembers what happened to the first forty-seven. Expedition 47 left a note: bring more money. You lead a party of three, Nationalist side only, from the Bund to the last pier: explore 3D streets, pick up supplies, rest at tea stoves, and touch an enemy to enter a turn-based battle.

**The expedition**

- **Lao Wang**: a conscript with a dadao. Big Sword Chop, Curse Roar (party damage up), Whirlwind of Debt, Last Stand, Back Pay Blitz (one hit for every month unpaid).
- **Little Blue**: a propagandist with a megaphone. Good News Barrage, Field Dressing, Loyalty Questionnaire (40 pages), New Life Movement (revive and heal the party; tuck in your shirt), Victory Newsreel (footage from 1938).
- **Accountant Qian**: a bookkeeper with a rifle. Aimed Shot, Audit, Printing Press, Hyperinflation (spends all stacked Inflation at once), Currency Reform (strike off three zeros).

**Six chapters** (each one opens with a pixel story page: a date, a place, one historical fact, one joke)

1. **The Bund, Shanghai**: black market touts, pickpockets, police carrying doors, rice hoarders and a hungry supply crate. Boss: **the Hoarder King** (black market, white gloves; bribes the police).
2. **Village of the Roped**: liberated twice, conscripted three times. Hollow conscripts, pitchfork militia, riflemen, underground agitators, grenadiers. Boss: **Commissar Wei**, Self-Criticism Enforcer (struggle sessions, rectification).
3. **The Bridge Nobody Blew Up**: the demolition budget was paid in Gold Yuan. Military police, riflemen, grenadiers. Boss: **the Iron General**, keeper of the locked bridge (his dadao catches fire at half health).
4. **The Mint That Never Sleeps**: printing a note costs more than the note. Mint clerks, walking stacks of notes and a hungry bank vault. Boss: **Treasurer Zhao** (Freeze Your Accounts drains AP; Emergency Issue at half health).
5. **Huaihai**: snowbound trenches; half a million of ours went the other way. Human waves, mortar teams, riflemen. Boss: **Colonel Lu**, of Flexible Loyalty (defects mid-fight: green cap, same sword).
6. **Docks of the Last Ferry**: a rainy pier. Customs inspectors, stevedores, military police. Final boss: **the Printer** (paints "48" on a party member, who is erased four turns later unless they dodge or parry to smudge the number; hyperinflation at half health).

**Progression and items**: battles give XP and levels (more HP and attack, new skills from level 2 to 8). The map holds rice wine, tiger balm, American aid tins and eight charms (ration coupon, Lend-Lease watch, cooking pot, KMT membership card, accountant's abacus, surplus M1 helmet, plus a lucky Gold Yuan and a victory bond carried by the two hungry chests). Every boss drops a chapter keepsake. Tea stoves heal the party, sell supplies and let you travel between lit stoves, but every rest raises prices 15% and respawns the small fry. The game saves at every stove and after every battle; Continue on the title resumes.

**Battle mechanics**

- Turn order by speed (top left). Attack earns +1 AP; skills cost AP; Shoot costs 1 AP and gives a free-aim crosshair: hit the glowing wallet for a weak-point crit; items are free.
- Skills show a shrinking ring: press as the white ring meets the gold one for GOOD or PERFECT.
- Enemy turn: press Dodge (wider timing) or Parry (tighter, +1 AP) as each blow lands. Parry every hit of a move to counterattack.
- Every enemy has a break gauge; fill it to Break them (they lose a turn and take +50% damage). Breaking the Printer wipes every "48".
- Strike an enemy first while exploring for an ambush. A party wipe is a "retreat": back to the last stove with your Gold Yuan devalued by half.

**Ending**: the expedition breaks the Printer and the number stops growing... then the front line arrives, right on schedule. Everyone boards the boat anyway: the gold reserves left months ago, first class, in crates marked "documents"; Gold Yuan is not accepted as fare, only as ballast. In December 1949 the government retreats to Taiwan, officially a temporary relocation. Very temporary: Lao Wang does not unpack. "We will counterattack the mainland next year," every year, as the calendar keeps turning. Qian frames the last Gold Yuan note, the only investment that never lost value because it started at zero. Credits follow (neutral observer: a donkey; financial advice: a seagull), then the tally: chapters 6/6, bosses 6/6, civil wars won 0/1, THE END (temporarily).

**Controls**

- Keyboard and mouse: W A S D move, click the game to capture the mouse for looking (arrow keys also turn the camera), Shift run, E use (stoves, items, floor messages, boss fog), J or left click to strike first for an ambush. Battle: W S (or arrows) choose, J / Enter confirm, K / Esc back, A D change target, J at skill rings and to fire when aiming; on the enemy turn Space dodges and Shift parries. P pause, L language, M sound. Battle menus can also be clicked.
- Phone: play in landscape (portrait shows a rotate hint). Thumb-stick on the left half, drag the right half to look, STRIKE / RUN / USE buttons; in battle tap menu rows and targets, tap anywhere at a skill ring, and use the big DODGE / PARRY buttons on the enemy turn. Top-right buttons switch language (EN / 中文), pause and toggle sound.
