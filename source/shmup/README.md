# 金圓長空 1949 Sky of Gold Yuan 1949

類型 Genre: 橫向捲軸射擊 Side-scrolling shoot 'em up · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `index.html`

## 中文

1948–49年。你開著一架借來的雙翼機替國軍空軍賣命，合約按時計酬，以金圓券支付。共軍在地面上節節勝利，不知怎麼的，連天上也是：對面開的是倒戈的飛機、木帆船、高射砲，還有一艘專門印鈔票的飛艇。你每一場空戰都會打贏，然後看看這有什麼用。

**六個關卡**（每關之前都有一頁像素劇情，開場有任務簡報，過關有結算：酬勞、米價、你的薪水能買幾粒米）

1. **淮海平原**（1948年11月，起霧的鄉間）：戰鬥機、俯衝機、氣球、高射砲、撒傳單的運輸機。魔王：裝甲列車「六任東家」，已經換過六次邊。
2. **雪中山口**（1949年1月，下雪）：新敵人「倒戈機」，塗著國軍顏色、紅漆還沒乾，從你背後飛來。魔王：轟炸機「三心二意」，會投下落地開花的炸彈。
3. **萬里長江**（1949年4月，夕陽江面）：新敵人木帆船，從江面往上開火。魔王：砲艇「新官上任」，環形彈幕。
4. **上海夜空**（1949年5月，外灘夜雨）：新敵人探照燈高射砲，順著光柱掃射。魔王：高射砲塔「夜班」，雙光柱加空爆彈。
5. **雲層之上**（1949年5月，天上飄鈔票）：新敵人「印鈔小飛艇」和會追人的鈔票。魔王：印鈔機飛艇，邊飛邊撒錢，第二階段還會「發行新鈔」。
6. **最後的船團**（1949年12月，台灣海峽，夜轉黎明）：護送船團，魚雷艇會直接撞上去。魔王：巡洋艦「前任旗艦」，砲擊船團也砲擊你。

**道具與機制**

- **P** 火力提升（最多四級：單發、三向、五向、加重彈）；被擊落會掉一級。**B** 金圓炸彈 +1。**¥** 加 500 分（約一粒米）。
- **金圓炸彈**：清空畫面上的子彈並重創所有敵人，畫面下起鈔票雨。不值錢，但很重。
- 分數以金圓券計算，累積到 3 萬、8 萬、16 萬、28 萬會「加聘一名飛行員（付金圓券）」。
- 每關魔王血量剩一半時會進入第二階段。第 6 關有船團耐久度，結算時顯示貨物送達百分比（零的話：還好黃金早就先走了）。
- 飛行員用完就「合約終止」：十秒內可以簽新合約原地接關，次數不限，最後會統計你簽了幾份合約。
- 過關自動存檔，可從「飛行日誌」選任何已解鎖的關卡，破關後可重看結局。暫停選單可重打本關、選關、切換語言。

**結局**：六戰六勝，總部發來賀電（收件人付費，發自廣州，不對，重慶，不對，成都）。與此同時，地面上其他的一切都輸光了，作戰地圖整片變紅，總部說「一切按照計畫」。最後一道命令：飛往台灣，單程，油錢以金圓券支付；加油加到一半匯率變了兩次，只加到半桶，最後十英里用滑翔的，降落時黃金已經在跑道上等你，它永遠先到。政府遷到台北，暫時的，非常暫時；你的酬勞用金圓券付清，機場收下來燒爐子。之後每年簡報都一樣：明年反攻大陸，日曆一路翻到今天。最後是片尾名單和戰績：過關 6／6、擊落魔王 6／6、簽過的合約份數、最終分數（實際價值：一顆蛋）、打贏的內戰 0／1，劇終（暫時）。

**操作**

- 鍵盤：WASD 或方向鍵飛行，J／空白鍵／Z 射擊（按住），K／X 金圓炸彈，P 或 Esc 暫停，L 切換語言，M 音效，Enter 翻頁與確認；飛行日誌按 1–6 選關。
- 手機：橫向遊玩。在畫面任何地方拖曳來飛行（像觸控板一樣相對移動），機槍自動射擊，右下圓形按鈕丟金圓炸彈；點一下翻頁。右上角可切換語言（EN／中文）、暫停、開關音效。直向時會提示把手機轉橫。

## English

1948–49. You fly a borrowed biplane for the Nationalist (KMT) Air Force, on a contract paid by the hour in Gold Yuan. The Communists are winning on the ground and, somehow, in the air: defected planes, wooden junks, flak guns and an airship that prints money. You will win every dogfight. See how much that helps.

**The six stages** (each one opens with a pixel-art story page and a mission briefing, and closes with a tally: pay, the price of rice, and how many grains your pay buys)

1. **The Northern Plain** (November 1948, hazy countryside): fighters, dive-bombers, balloons, AA guns, leaflet transports. Boss: armored train "Six Owners", which has changed sides six times.
2. **The Snow Pass** (January 1949, snow): new enemy, turncoat planes in Nationalist colours (the red paint is still wet) that attack from behind. Boss: bomber "Second Thoughts", whose bombs burst on the ground.
3. **The Yangtze** (April 1949, sunset river): new enemy, junks firing up from the water. Boss: gunboat "New Management", ring barrages.
4. **Shanghai Skyline** (May 1949, the Bund in night rain): new enemy, searchlight flak firing along the beams. Boss: flak tower "Night Shift", twin beams and air-burst shells.
5. **Above the Clouds** (May 1949, raining banknotes): new enemies, printing blimps and banknotes that chase you. Boss: the Printer's airship, which sprays money and, in phase two, "issues new notes".
6. **The Last Convoy** (December 1949, Taiwan Strait, night into dawn): escort the convoy while torpedo boats try to ram it. Boss: cruiser "Formerly Our Flagship", shelling the convoy and you.

**Power-ups and mechanics**

- **P** more guns (four levels: single, 3-way, 5-way, heavy rounds); you lose one level when shot down. **B** one more money bomb. **¥** 500 points (about one grain of rice).
- **Money bomb**: clears enemy bullets and hits everything on screen under a rain of banknotes. Worthless, but heavy.
- Score is in Gold Yuan; at 30K, 80K, 160K and 280K an extra pilot is hired (paid in Gold Yuan).
- Every boss enters a second phase at half health. Stage 6 has a convoy health bar; the tally reports the cargo delivered (if it is zero: the gold left earlier anyway).
- Out of pilots means CONTRACT TERMINATED: sign a new contract within ten seconds to continue on the spot, as often as you like. The ending counts your contracts.
- Progress saves after every stage. The Flight Log lets you replay any unlocked stage and rewatch the ending. The pause menu restarts the stage, opens stage select or switches language.

**Ending**: six for six. HQ sends congratulations by telegram, collect, from Canton, no, Chongqing, no, Chengdu. Meanwhile, on the ground, everything else was lost; the HQ map turns red while an officer says it is all according to plan. Last orders: fly to Taiwan, one way, fuel paid in Gold Yuan. The rate changes twice while they fill the tank, so you get half a tank and glide the last ten miles; the gold is already waiting on the runway, because it always leaves first. The government relocates to Taipei, temporarily, very temporarily, and your fee is paid in Gold Yuan, which the airfield burns in the stove. Every year after that the briefing is the same, counterattack the mainland next year, while the calendar flips all the way to today. Credits and a tally close it out: stages 6/6, bosses 6/6, contracts signed, final score (real value: one egg), civil wars won 0/1, THE END (temporarily).

**Controls**

- Keyboard: WASD or arrows fly, J / Space / Z fire (hold), K / X money bomb, P or Esc pause, L language, M sound, Enter advances pages; 1–6 pick a stage in the Flight Log.
- Phone: play in landscape. Drag anywhere to fly (relative, like a trackpad), the guns fire on their own, the round button bottom right drops a money bomb; tap to turn pages. Top-right buttons switch language (EN / 中文), pause and toggle sound. Portrait shows a hint to turn the phone sideways.
