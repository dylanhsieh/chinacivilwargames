# 壯丁深淵 Conscript's Descent

類型 Genre: 動作角色扮演／類 Rogue 地下城 Action RPG / roguelike dungeon crawler (Diablo style) · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `conscripts-descent.html`

## 中文

1948 到 1949 年，你是國軍的一名壯丁，從燒毀的城郊一路往下打，經過戰壕、上海下水道和央行的金庫地道，最後殺上外灘。一路見什麼搶什麼。薪餉用金圓券發，數字每層都在漲，價值每層都在跌（HUD 會順便換算成雞蛋）。死了就沒了，一條命打完六層。你每一仗都會贏，然後看看這有什麼用。

**六層樓**（開場有一頁 1946 年停戰的像素劇情，第三層打完再一頁 1948 年金圓券改革）

1. **城郊**：燒毀的村子。總部提醒：搜刮要有責任感，長官先搜。
2. **戰壕**：兩邊一起挖的戰壕，挖到中間碰頭還一起吃了午飯。開始出現狙擊手。
3. **村口廣場**（頭目）：**多主戰車**。會發射砲彈（地上紅圈是落點）、機槍掃射、全速衝撞（借來的），血量每掉一截就換一次車主：美國陸軍、大日本帝國、國軍、共軍、某軍閥、沒人（暫時）。半血後還會叫衝鋒兵支援。打贏之後重新烤漆已排程，又一次。
4. **上海下水道**：下面的鈔票跟上面的鈔票一樣值錢，意思你懂。騎兵登場。
5. **金庫地道**：央行黃金從這裡運走，官方說法是這批黃金從來不存在。跟著拖痕走。
6. **外灘**（最終頭目）：**偉大領袖（臉部待核准）**，一塊會走路的巨大看板。會踩出衝擊波、用眼睛掃射雷射、一圈一圈放出口號彈幕，還會不斷叫援軍。打倒之後你會發現，原來是一面鏡子。一直都是鏡子。

每兩層之間是**野戰營地**：士氣回滿、米酒補滿，軍需官擺出四件貨（我們的物資，他的價錢），也收購你背包裡的東西。

**三種職業**（技能在 1、2、3、5 級解鎖，數字鍵 1–4 施放，消耗「熱忱」）

- **大刀隊**（近戰 · 耐打）：大刀砍；大刀迴旋斬（按住持續轉）、衝出戰壕（跳過去砸下，暈眩）、國罵怒吼（傷害 +50%，把敵人嚇跑）、集束餿主意（六顆手榴彈綁一捆）。
- **神槍手**（遠程 · 靈活）：步槍射擊；齊射、地雷（敵人會自願踩上去）、戰略轉進（翻滾時無敵）、湯姆生彈幕（三秒鐘借來的美援火力）。
- **宣傳員**（施法 · 召喚）：撒傳單（會自己追敵人）；口號轟炸（暈眩，偶爾讓敵人投誠）、通膨新星（鈔票炸開，一文不值但很利）、招降納叛（兩個敵兵暫時換邊）、美援空投（砸敵人，站旁邊回血）。

**戰利品與機制**

- 裝備五個欄位：武器、頭部、身體、腳、護身符。分普通、魔法、稀有、傳說四種等級，詞綴像「通膨」「長冬」「貪污」「自省」……
- 十件傳說裝備，例如金圓券鎖子甲（身上越有錢護甲越高，只要你永遠不花它）、戰略轉進之靴、家書、那口炒菜鍋（25% 機率擋子彈）、多主大刀、真理大聲公（待核准）。
- 撿到更好的東西會自動裝備；背包 20 格，戰場上可以「拆了換錢」，營地裡賣原價。
- 升級時三選一升官卡（加菜、美援子彈、黑市人脈、高效官僚……外加各職業專屬卡）。剩下的給長官的姪子。
- 米酒壺（F）回復 45% 士氣，每 20 秒補一壺。士氣就是生命值。
- 敵人：步槍兵、刺刀衝鋒兵、擲彈兵、政委（替身邊敵人打氣，打完仗要自我批評）、迫擊砲、狙擊手、騎兵、會射口號的擴音喇叭（口號可以打掉）。精英隊伍帶詞綴：快腿、自我批評（回血）、會爆炸、通膨（更大更肥，掉五倍錢）、長征（不會被擊退或暈眩）、受傷就投誠、大嗓門。還有帶名字的稀有隊長，例如「很餓的傅軍需」。
- 地圖上有四種神壇（通膨、自我批評、戰略轉進、黨綱）、寫著「美援」的物資箱（內容物已被偷走一部分）、被綁的農民（救了會送你米酒）、會爆炸的油桶。
- 被打到士氣太低的敵人可能投誠，幫你打一陣子再回家。

**結局**：六層全勝，戰爭全輸。你爬出地面，發現政府已經丟了東北、南京和上海，而你是全年唯一打贏過任何東西的單位（請保密，會破壞整體敘事）。1949 年 12 月，政府宣布「暫時遷移」到台灣，黃金先坐頭等艙走了；你拿全部戰利品換船票，事務長只收你的米酒。1950 年元旦在台北，總部承諾明年就反攻大陸。1951、1952、1953 年都是明年，你的背包開始長香菇。最後是戰績卡：從未戰敗，照樣撤退；抵台價值：一壺米酒。

**操作**

- 滑鼠：左鍵點地面移動、點敵人攻擊（按住可持續）；右鍵放技能 1。
- 鍵盤：W A S D／方向鍵走路，空白鍵或 J 攻擊最近的敵人，1–4 技能，F 喝米酒，I 背包，P 或 Esc 暫停，L 切換語言（中文／English）。
- 手機：橫向遊玩。左半邊螢幕任意處按住當搖桿，右下「攻擊」、1–4 技能和「米酒」按鈕會自動瞄準最近的敵人。上方按鈕可切換語言、暫停、開背包、開關音效。點一下畫面可跳過劇情。

## English

1948–49. You are a Nationalist (KMT) conscript fighting your way down six floors, from the burning outskirts through the trenches, the Shanghai sewers and the central bank's vault tunnels, up onto the Bund. Loot everything. Your pay is in Gold Yuan: the number goes up every floor and the value goes down (the HUD helpfully converts it to eggs). Permadeath, one life, six floors. You will win every fight. See how much that helps.

**The six floors** (a pixel story page opens the run with the 1946 truce, and another follows floor 3 with the 1948 Gold Yuan reform)

1. **The Outskirts**: a burning village. HQ reminds you to loot responsibly. Officers loot first.
2. **The Trenches**: both sides dug them, met in the middle and had lunch. Snipers start to appear.
3. **The Village Square** (boss): **the Tank of Many Owners**. It shells you (red circles mark where), sprays machine-gun bursts and rams at "borrowed" speed, and every chunk of damage transfers ownership: US Army, Imperial Japan, Nationalist Army, People's Army, a warlord, nobody (briefly). Below half health it calls in bayonet runners. Afterwards: repaint scheduled. Again.
4. **Shanghai Sewers**: the money down there is worth exactly as much as the money up here. Cavalry joins in.
5. **The Vault Tunnels**: the central bank's gold went through here. Officially it never existed. Follow the drag marks.
6. **The Bund** (final boss): **the Great Leader (face pending approval)**, a giant walking billboard. Stomp shockwaves, a sweeping eye laser, rings of slogan bullets and endless reinforcements. Beat it and it turns out to be a mirror. It was always a mirror.

Between floors is the **field camp**: morale and rice wine refilled, a quartermaster with four items (our supplies, his prices) who also buys from your bag.

**Three classes** (skills unlock at levels 1, 2, 3 and 5, keys 1–4, paid for in zeal)

- **Bayonet Brute** (melee, tough): dadao slash; Big Sword Spin (hold to keep spinning), Over the Top (leap and crash down, stuns), MTFK Roar (+50% damage, enemies flee), Bundle of Bad Ideas (six grenades tied together).
- **Sharpshooter** (ranged, nimble): rifle shot; Volley, Landmine (enemies volunteer to step on it), Strategic Withdrawal (invulnerable roll), Tommy Barrage (three seconds of borrowed American firepower).
- **Propagandist** (caster, summons): homing pamphlets; Slogan Blast (stuns, sometimes converts), Inflation Nova (banknotes explode outward: worthless, but sharp), Recruit Defectors (two enemies switch sides for a while), American Aid (a crate falls on enemies and heals you nearby).

**Loot and mechanics**

- Five slots: weapon, head, body, feet, charm. Common, magic, rare and legendary items with affixes like Inflated, of the Long Winter, Corrupt, Self-Critical…
- Ten legendaries, such as the Gold Yuan Brigandine (armor grows with the gold you carry, bulletproof as long as you never spend it), Boots of Strategic Withdrawal, Letter from Home, The Cooking Pot (25% chance to block bullets), Dadao of Many Owners and the Megaphone of Truth (Pending Approval).
- Clear upgrades equip themselves; the bag holds 20; scrap for gold in the field or sell at full price in camp.
- Level-ups offer three promotion cards (Extra Rations, Lend-Lease Ammo, Black Market Contacts, Efficient Bureaucracy… plus class cards). The others go to an officer's nephew.
- Rice wine (F) restores 45% morale; one flask refills every 20 seconds. Morale is your health.
- Enemies: riflemen, bayonet runners, grenadiers, commissars (they buff everyone nearby, self-criticism after the battle), mortars, snipers, cavalry and loudspeakers that fire slogans (you can shoot the slogans down). Champion packs carry affixes: Fast, Self-Critical (regenerates), Explosive, Inflated (bigger, five times the gold), Long March (no knockback or stun), Defects When Hurt, Loud. Named rare captains such as "Hungry Quartermaster Fu" lead their own squads.
- Floors hold four kinds of shrine (Inflation, Self-Criticism, Strategic Withdrawal, the Party Line), US AID crates (contents partially stolen), tied-up prisoners who give you rice wine, and explosive barrels.
- Beaten-down enemies sometimes defect and fight for you for a while before going home.

**Ending**: six floors won, the war lost. You climb out to learn the government has lost Manchuria, Nanjing and Shanghai, and that you are the only unit that won anything all year (please keep it quiet, it ruins the narrative). In December 1949 the government announces a "temporary relocation" to Taiwan; the gold left months ago, first class, and the purser takes your rice wine instead of your loot. New Year 1950 in Taipei: HQ promises to counterattack the mainland next year. 1951, 1952, 1953: next year. Your kit bag grows mushrooms. The end card reads UNDEFEATED. EVACUATED. Value on arrival: 1 rice wine.

**Controls**

- Mouse: left click the ground to walk, click an enemy to attack (hold to keep going); right click for skill 1.
- Keyboard: W A S D / arrows walk, Space or J attacks the nearest enemy, 1–4 skills, F rice wine, I bag, P or Esc pause, L switches language (中文 / English).
- Phone: play in landscape. Touch and hold anywhere on the left half for a joystick; ATTACK, the 1–4 skill buttons and WINE auto-aim at the nearest enemy. Top buttons switch language, pause, open the bag and toggle sound. Tap to skip story pages.
