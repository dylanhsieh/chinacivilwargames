# 黑暗金圓 Dark Yuan

類型 Genre: 2D 橫向卷軸類魂動作 2D side-scrolling souls-like (Dark Souls / Salt and Sanctuary style) · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `dark-yuan.html`

## 中文

1948 年，中國。沒拿到退伍令就戰死的兵，會爬起來繼續打。你是一名國軍壯丁，死在用繩子綁成一串的押送隊伍裡，然後又爬了起來，班長照樣替你點名。共軍要你死，自己的長官要你歸隊。你要一路砍到最後一班渡輪，湊齊退伍令上的兩顆印章。武器是一把大刀和一個鍋蓋，薪餉是金圓券，而金圓券每休息一次就貶值一次。

**五個區域、五個頭目**（區域之間沒有讀取畫面，一條路從村口走到碼頭；每個頭目都在白霧後面等你）

1. **綁繩之村**：燃燒的村莊、抓兵隊和拿草叉的農民。頭目 **魏政委**（自我批評督導員）：會揮刀連砍，還會朝你丟出「自我批評！」「批鬥大會！」的口號。掉落「自我批評書，四十頁」（手榴彈 +1）。
2. **沒人炸掉的橋**：河上的斷橋、深水坑和狙擊手。頭目 **馬將軍**（逃兵處決者）：三連斬、跳劈、衝鋒，半血之後大刀著火、開始旋轉。掉落「將軍的印章」（退伍令的一半）。
3. **不眠的印鈔廠**：天上飄著鈔票，盾兵守著櫃檯。頭目 **趙司庫**（印鈔機的守護者）：體型巨大，用一袋金圓券砸地，噴出一疊疊「¥1,000,000」「新鈔發行！」。打倒他之後橫幅寫著：「不過，通膨依然無敵」。掉落百萬元大鈔印版（大刀傷害 +12%）。
4. **淮海：大軍一去不回**：雪地戰壕，還有魏政委的替身當小頭目。頭目 **盧上校**（忠誠非常有彈性）：拿長槍突刺，半血時當場投共，帽子變綠色，長槍倒是沒換。掉落雙面軍帽（一面藍一面綠，士氣上限 +15%）。
5. **末班渡輪碼頭**：下雨的碼頭、騙子和警察。最終頭目 **兄弟倆**：迅捷的紅老弟（共軍）和壯碩的藍大哥（國軍，拿著一袋錢）。打倒其中一個，另一個會吸收他、補滿血，變成「統一戰線（暫時性）」，專門對付你一個。掉落統一戰線的印章，退伍令終於完整。

**戰鬥、休息與通膨**

- 輕擊三連段、重擊（破盾、破架勢）、翻滾（途中無敵）、防禦（鍋蓋）。在攻擊落下的瞬間輕點防禦就是**彈反**，接著攻擊就是**處決**；從背後出手是**背刺**；從平台跳下時按「下＋攻擊」可以**下劈**。
- 子彈和口號也能彈反，原件退回。頭目的架勢被打崩時同樣可以處決。精力（綠條）決定你能揮幾刀、滾幾次。
- **茶爐**就是篝火：休息會補滿生命、米酒壺和手榴彈，敵人全部復活，**物價上漲 15%**。在茶爐用金圓券升級生命力、耐力、力量，也能在點燃過的茶爐之間移動。
- 你身上的金圓券每休息一次就貶值一次。升級價格、敵人掉的錢也跟著通膨。
- **死亡**：身上的金圓券全部掉在倒下的地方，而且躺在地上每秒再貶值 1%（最低剩兩成）。回去撿就拿得回來；途中再死一次，就永遠沒了，結算畫面會統計「被通膨吃掉的金圓券」。
- 地上的橘色留言是其他亡魂留下的提示（「前有很棒的政委」「試試轉進」「小心左派。還有右派。這是內戰。」）。有一個「美援」補給箱其實是會咬人的寶箱怪。
- 道具：配給券（米酒壺 +1）、磨刀石（傷害 +12%）、手榴彈袋、棉背心（1947 年冬天訂的，1948 年夏天到貨）、一整磚金圓券。進度自動存檔，可從標題「繼續」。

**結局**：帶著兩顆印章登上最後一班渡輪。三頁劇情：共軍拿下大陸，國庫黃金搭船先走；你的欠餉是一皮箱金圓券，可以買一顆橘子，而且那顆橘子還被高估了；「明年反攻大陸！」每年宣布一次，你在 1987 年退伍，行李到現在還沒拆。畫面打出「你轉進了」（撤退 · 暫時的 · 非常暫時的），附上時間、死亡次數、擊倒敵人、被通膨吃掉的金圓券、靈魂等級。「反攻大陸（NG+）」開始二周目：保留能力值，敵人更硬、更痛、掉更多錢。

**操作**

- 鍵盤：A D 移動 · 空白鍵 跳 · J 攻擊 · U 重擊 · K 翻滾 · L 防禦（輕點彈反）· R 喝米酒 · G 丟手榴彈 · W / E 使用（休息、撿東西、讀留言、走進白霧）· S＋空白鍵 從平台跳下 · P / Esc 暫停。標題畫面和結局按 L 切換語言。
- 滑鼠：左鍵攻擊，右鍵防禦。
- 手機：橫向遊玩。左手拇指在螢幕左半邊任意處拖曳當搖桿，右邊按鈕是攻擊、重擊、翻滾、跳、防禦／彈反、米酒、手雷；可以互動時中間下方會出現「休息」「撿起」等按鈕。點一下畫面跳過劇情。右上角可切換語言（EN／中文）、暫停、開關音效。

## English

China, 1948. Soldiers who die without discharge papers get back up and keep fighting. You are a Nationalist (KMT) conscript who died in a roped column of press-ganged farmers; you got up, and the sergeant marked you present. The Communists want you dead, your own officers want you back. Cut your way to the last ferry and collect both stamps for your discharge papers. You carry a dadao and a pot lid, you are paid in Gold Yuan, and your Gold Yuan loses value every time you rest.

**The five areas and five bosses** (one continuous road from the village to the docks; every boss waits behind a wall of fog)

1. **Village of the Roped**: a burning village, press gangs and farmers with pitchforks. Boss: **Commissar Wei, Self-Criticism Enforcer**, who chains sword combos and throws slogans at you ("SELF-CRITICIZE!", "STRUGGLE SESSION!"). Drops *Self-Criticism, 40 Pages* (+1 grenade).
2. **The Bridge Nobody Blew Up**: a broken bridge, deep water and snipers. Boss: **General Ma, Executioner of Deserters**: three-hit combos, leaping slams and charges; below half health his dadao catches fire and he starts spinning. Drops *The General's Stamp*, half of your discharge papers.
3. **The Mint That Never Sleeps**: banknotes falling like snow, shield bearers at the counters. Boss: **Treasurer Zhao, Keeper of the Presses**, a giant who slams a sack of Gold Yuan and fires stacks of "¥1,000,000" and "NEW ISSUE!" at you. His defeat banner: "INFLATION, HOWEVER, IS UNDEFEATED". Drops the *One-Million Note Plate* (+12% damage).
4. **Huaihai, Where the Army Went**: snowy trenches, plus Commissar Wei's understudy as a mini-boss. Boss: **Colonel Lu, of Flexible Loyalty**, a spearman who defects mid-fight at half health: his cap turns green, he keeps the same spear. Drops the *Reversible Cap* (blue on one side, green on the other; +15% max morale).
5. **Docks of the Last Ferry**: rain, liars and police. Final boss: **the Brothers**, Brother Red the Swift (Communist, spear) and Brother Blue the Stout (Nationalist, sack of money). Kill one and the other absorbs him, refills his health and becomes **The United Front (Temporary)**, united against you, specifically. Drops *The United Front's Stamp*; your discharge papers are complete.

**Combat, resting and inflation**

- Three-hit light combo, heavy attack (breaks shields and poise), roll (invincible mid-roll) and guard with the pot lid. Tap guard just as a blow lands to **parry**, then attack to **riposte**; strike from behind to **backstab**; down + attack in the air to **plunge**.
- Bullets and slogans can be parried too: return to sender. A boss whose poise breaks can also be riposted. Stamina (the green bar) limits how much you swing and roll.
- **Tea stoves** are the bonfires: resting refills health, rice wine and grenades, respawns every enemy, and **raises prices 15%**. Spend Gold Yuan at a stove to level Vigor, Endurance and Strength, and travel between lit stoves.
- Yuan you carry loses value every rest; level costs and enemy drops inflate with it.
- **Death**: all your Gold Yuan drops where you fell and keeps inflating away on the ground, 1% a second (down to a fifth). Walk back to recover it; die on the way and it is gone for good, counted on the final screen as "Gold Yuan lost to inflation".
- Orange messages on the ground are hints from other dead soldiers ("AMAZING COMMISSAR AHEAD", "TRY RETREATING", "BE WARY OF LEFT. AND RIGHT. IT IS A CIVIL WAR."). One US AID crate is a mimic.
- Items: ration tickets (+1 rice wine flask), sharpening stones (+12% damage), grenade pouches, a padded vest (ordered winter 1947, delivered summer 1948), bricks of Gold Yuan. Progress saves automatically; Continue from the title.

**Ending**: board the last ferry with both stamps. Three story cards: the Communists take the mainland and the gold reserves leave first, by ship; your back pay is a suitcase of Gold Yuan that buys one overvalued tangerine; "we counterattack the mainland next year!" is announced every year, and you are discharged in 1987 without ever unpacking. Then "YOU RETREATED" (temporarily, very temporarily) with your time, deaths, kills, Gold Yuan lost to inflation and soul level. "Counterattack (NG+)" starts the next cycle with your stats kept and tougher, harder-hitting, better-paying enemies.

**Controls**

- Keyboard: A D move · Space jump · J attack · U heavy · K roll · L guard (tap to parry) · R rice wine · G grenade · W / E use (rest, pick up, read, enter the fog) · S + Space drop through a platform · P / Esc pause. L switches language on the title and ending screens.
- Mouse: left click attack, right click guard.
- Phone: play in landscape. Drag anywhere on the left half as a joystick; the buttons on the right are attack, heavy, roll, jump, guard/parry, wine and bomb, and a context button (REST, PICK UP…) appears at the bottom when you can interact. Tap to skip story text. Top-right buttons switch language (EN / 中文), pause and toggle sound.
