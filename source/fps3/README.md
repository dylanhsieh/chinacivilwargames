# 國共大戰：圍村 Civil Slug: Village Siege

類型 Genre: 第一人稱 3D 像素射擊 First-person 3D pixel shooter (Three.js, Doom / Wolfenstein style) · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `civil-slug-siege.html`

## 中文

1948 到 1949 年，你是一個國軍壯丁，薪餉用金圓券發，而且每十秒依通膨「往下」調整一次。從一個起火的村子一路打到最後一個碼頭，六關、六個頭目，每一關之前和之後都有一頁像素劇情，寫的是真實的日期和史實，配上很黑的註解。你每一仗都會打贏，然後還是得上船去台灣。暫時的。

**六關與頭目**

1. **圍村之戰**：解救被綁的村民、讓共軍的擴音喇叭閉嘴，小心屋頂上的狙擊手。頭目「多主戰車」：每打掉一截血，車牌上的「所屬」就換一次（美國陸軍、大日本帝國、國軍、共軍、某軍閥、沒人）。
2. **鐵路線**（雨天）：穿越稻田，幫被綁在籬笆樁上的農民鬆綁。頭目「裝甲列車」：1937 年來首度準點，兩座砲塔沿著鐵軌追著你打。
3. **冰封山口**（東北、下雪、零下三十度、沒有冬衣）：守住山口。頭目「轟炸機」：在頭頂繞圈，飛近時丟炸彈，燃料大部分是水。
4. **長江防線**（1949 年 4 月，夜晚）：死守江岸。頭目「忠誠度可調砲艇」：一邊開砲一邊換旗子，青天白日、紅旗、白旗（未決定）。
5. **上海**（1949 年 5 月，天上下鈔票）：物價每小時翻倍。頭目「印鈔機」：朝你噴出一疊疊會追人的「¥10,000,000」和「金圓券！」。
6. **最後的碼頭**（1949 年 12 月）：最後一班船今天開，總部已經在船上督戰。最終頭目「會走路的看板（臉部待核准）」：會踩人、會喊口號、會開砲，臉改了又改，還是沒核准。

**武器與機制**

- 武器：駁殼槍（無限子彈）、湯姆生衝鋒槍、巴祖卡火箭筒，加上手榴彈；敵人貼身時開火會自動拔刀砍。武器沒子彈時自動換回駁殼槍。
- 敵人：步槍兵、上刺刀衝鋒的（「三個人一把槍！」）、擲彈兵、軍官（掉出一箱黃金＋赴台船票）、機槍巢、屋頂狙擊手（開槍前會反光）。舉手投降的不要打：打了扣 500 分（打仗也是要寫報告的）；走過去他就投誠。
- 擴音喇叭桿（掛著「解放」紅布條）會發射會追人的口號；口號可以開槍「駁斥」，喇叭打爆有獎金。
- 被綁的村民：走近或開槍打斷繩子就能鬆綁，每救一個會留下補給（手榴彈、黑市白米、衝鋒槍、巴祖卡），還會跟你抱怨兩邊軍隊。
- 薪餉（分數）以金圓券計，數字一直變大，換算成雞蛋一直變少。每關結算：擊倒敵軍、解救村民、擊毀喇叭、消耗壯丁（零損失有一萬獎金），評價從「（正確的）人民英雄」到「統計數字」。
- 一開始有兩個備用壯丁，分數夠高會再「送來」一個。壯丁用完可以「再抓三個壯丁」從頭目前的檢查點繼續，或重打這一關。破關進度自動存檔，標題畫面可以接續。

**結局**：六戰全勝，戰爭全輸。「同一時間，1949 年」：其他的仗政府全輸了，總部把你的戰績蓋上「絕對機密」。「最後一班船」：國庫黃金幾個月前就坐頭等艙先走了，你的金圓券薪餉只買得到站位，戰車留在岸上等第七任車主。「1950 年元旦，台北」：明年就反攻大陸；1951 年也是明年，1952 年也是，講稿現在都提前印好。最後是戰績卡：戰役勝場 6／6，戰爭勝場 0／1，按鈕寫著「反攻大陸（明年）」。

**操作**

- 鍵盤＋滑鼠：W A S D 移動，點一下畫面鎖定滑鼠瞄準（方向鍵也能轉頭、抬頭），滑鼠左鍵／J／Enter 開火（貼身自動用刀），空白鍵或 K 跳，G 或 L 丟手榴彈，Q 或 E 換槍，1 2 3 直接選武器，P／Esc 暫停。劇情頁按 J、空白鍵或點滑鼠繼續。
- 手機：橫向遊玩（直向會提示轉橫）。左手拇指按住畫面左半邊就是搖桿，右手拇指在右半邊拖曳往任何方向瞄準；「開火」「手榴彈」「換槍」「跳」四個按鈕在右下。點一下畫面跳過劇情。
- 上方按鈕可隨時切換語言（EN／中文，暫停中也可以）、暫停、開關音效；標題畫面也有語言按鈕。語言設定會記住。

## English

1948 to 1949. You are one Nationalist (KMT) conscript, paid in Gold Yuan, and your pay is adjusted for inflation every ten seconds. Downward. You fight from a burning village to the last pier: six stages, six bosses, and a pixel-art story page before and after each stage with a real date, a real fact and a dark caption. You will win every battle. Then you get on the boat to Taiwan anyway. Temporarily.

**The six stages and bosses**

1. **Village Siege**: free the tied-up villagers, silence the enemy loudspeakers and watch the rooftops for snipers. Boss: the Tank of Many Owners. Every chunk of damage changes the "PROPERTY OF" plate (US Army, Imperial Japan, Nationalist Army, People's Army, a warlord, nobody, briefly).
2. **The Railway** (rain): cross the paddies and untie the farmers from the fence posts. Boss: the Armored Train, first on schedule since 1937, two turrets chasing you along the track.
3. **The Mountain Pass** (Manchuria, snow, minus 30, no coats): hold the pass. Boss: the Bomber, circling overhead and bombing you when it passes close. Its fuel is mostly water.
4. **The Yangtze** (April 1949, night): hold the riverbank. Boss: the Gunboat of Flexible Loyalty, which shells you while switching flags: Nationalist, red, white (undecided).
5. **Shanghai** (May 1949, raining banknotes): prices double every hour. Boss: the Printing Press, spraying homing "¥10,000,000" and "GOLD YUAN!" notes at you.
6. **The Last Pier** (December 1949): the last boat leaves today and HQ is supervising from it. Final boss: the Walking Billboard (Face Pending Approval). It stomps, shouts slogans and fires a cannon. The face gets revised. It is still not approved.

**Weapons and mechanics**

- Weapons: the C96 Mauser (infinite ammo), the Tommy gun and the bazooka, plus grenades; firing at an enemy right next to you uses the knife. An empty weapon drops you back to the Mauser.
- Enemies: riflemen, bayonet chargers ("one rifle per three men!"), grenadiers, officers (who drop a suitcase of gold and one ticket to Taiwan), machine-gun nests and rooftop snipers (who glint before they fire). Do not shoot the ones with their hands up: it costs 500 points (even this war has paperwork). Walk up to them and they defect.
- Loudspeaker poles (with a red 解放 "Liberation" banner) fire homing slogans. Shoot a slogan to refute it; destroy the pole for a bonus.
- Tied villagers: walk up or shoot the rope. Each one leaves supplies (grenades, black-market rice, Tommy gun, bazooka) and a complaint about both armies.
- Pay (score) is shown in Gold Yuan: the number keeps growing, its value in eggs keeps shrinking. Each stage ends with a tally: enemies dispatched, villagers untied, speakers silenced, conscripts spent (10,000 bonus for losing none), and a rank from "Hero of the (Correct) People" to "Statistic".
- You start with two spare conscripts, and a high enough score delivers another. Out of conscripts, "Draft 3 more" continues from the boss checkpoint, or restart the stage. Cleared stages save automatically and can be continued from the title.

**Ending**: six battles won, one war lost. "Meanwhile, 1949": the government lost all the other battles, and HQ stamps your record TOP SECRET. "The last boat": the gold reserves left months ago, first class; your Gold Yuan pay buys standing room, and the tank stays behind for owner number seven. "New Year, 1950, Taipei": the counterattack on the mainland is next year; in 1951 it is next year, and in 1952, and the speech is now printed in advance. A final card closes it: battles won 6 of 6, wars won 0 of 1, and a button that says "Counterattack (next year)".

**Controls**

- Keyboard and mouse: W A S D move, click the game to capture the mouse for aiming (arrow keys also turn and look up/down), left click / J / Enter fire (knife up close), Space or K jump, G or L grenade, Q or E swap weapon, 1 2 3 pick a weapon, P / Esc pause. On story pages, J, Space or a click continues.
- Phone: play in landscape (portrait shows a rotate hint). Put your left thumb anywhere on the left half for a thumb-stick, drag your right thumb on the right half to aim in any direction; FIRE, BOMB, SWAP and JUMP buttons sit bottom right. Tap the screen to skip story pages.
- The buttons at the top switch language (EN / 中文, also while paused), pause and toggle sound; the title screen has language buttons too. The language choice is remembered.
