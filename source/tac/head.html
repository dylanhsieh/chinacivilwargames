PAPER GENERALS1949 · TURN-BASED TACTICS · SIX BATTLES

Command a Nationalist squad through six battles in 1949, from a village well to the last ferry out. You pay in Gold Yuan; the enemy pays in leaflets. Soldiers whose morale runs out change sides, and so can yours. Win every battle. See how much that helps.

Click a soldier, click a blue tile to move, click a marked enemy to fire. Grenades, Rally and Leaflets are in the side panel.
Keys: arrows + Enter, Tab next soldier, G R L W for abilities, E end turn, Esc cancel.

Satire. Morale is a resource. So, technically, is the enemy. Progress saves automatically.
