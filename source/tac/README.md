# 紙上將軍 1949 Paper Generals 1949

類型 Genre: 回合制戰棋 Turn-based tactics · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `index.html`

## 中文

1949年，你指揮一支國軍小隊，在 12×8 的方格戰場上打完六場仗：從村口水井一路打到最後一班渡輪。你發的餉是金圓券，共軍發的是傳單。士氣歸零的士兵會直接換邊站，你的也一樣。你每一仗都會打贏，然後看看這有什麼用。

**六場戰役**（每場之前都有一頁「中央快報」像素劇情：頭版永遠在報捷，地圖上的紅色永遠在變多）

1. **村口水井**：從「土匪」手上搶下一口井（後來發現是乾的）。教學關，敵軍士氣動搖。
2. **稻田小路**：一挺機槍守著唯一一條乾的路，稻田難走，發餉也是。
3. **那座橋**：死守小橋 8 個回合，總部保證的援軍沒來，共軍的援軍有來。
4. **雪中山口**：「向後方前進」。只要讓一名士兵走到旗子那裡就算贏。
5. **最後一班飛機**（新增）：死守機場 7 回合，掩護運輸機裝上黃金、檔案和一架鋼琴。士兵座位請搭下一班。
6. **最後一班渡輪**（最終決戰）：共軍開著從國軍搶去的戰車佔領碼頭，還有援軍一波波趕來。總部說會幫你留船，總部人在船上。

**特殊機制**

- 士氣：中彈、戰友陣亡、被撒傳單都會掉士氣；士氣歸零的士兵會投誠到另一邊，敵人也一樣，可以策反過來。
- 軍需官：「發餉打氣」替身邊弟兄回士氣（獎金一百萬，約一顆包子）；「撒傳單」打擊敵軍士氣。
- 擲彈兵有兩顆手榴彈（十字範圍爆炸），機槍移動後命中率下降，戰車沒有士氣、不會投降。
- 掩體：竹林、房屋、沙包會降低命中率。每場有三顆星：勝利、零損失、在限定回合內獲勝。
- 軍餉每回合以金圓券計價，數字一直變大，價值一直變小。進度自動存檔。

**結局**：六戰全勝，戰爭卻輸了。總部很高興，也在打包；官方報告把一切稱為「戰略轉進」，蓋上「暫時」「非常」兩個章。小隊從剛打贏的碼頭登上最後一班渡輪撤往台灣，軍需官在船上補發一整年金圓券欠餉，裝滿兩麻袋，拿去跟海鷗換魚，海鷗拒絕。到了台灣（暫時），反攻大陸預定明年，每一年都是明年。最後是片尾名單和戰績統計：勝場 6／6，打贏的戰爭 0／1，星星限大陸兌換。

**操作**

- 滑鼠：點選士兵 → 點藍色格子移動 → 點框起來的敵人開火（顯示命中率）。手榴彈、發餉打氣、撒傳單、原地待命、結束回合都在右側面板。
- 鍵盤：方向鍵移動游標＋Enter 確認，Tab 下一位士兵，G 手榴彈、R 發餉打氣、L 撒傳單、W 待命，E 結束回合，Esc 取消；戰役地圖按 1–6 選關。
- 手機：橫向遊玩，直接點士兵、格子、敵人和右側面板按鈕。右上角可切換語言（EN／中文）、回到地圖、開關音效。

## English

1949. You command a Nationalist (KMT) squad on a 12×8 grid through six battles, from a village well to the last ferry out. You pay in Gold Yuan; the enemy pays in leaflets. Soldiers whose morale hits zero change sides, and so can yours. You will win every battle. See how much that helps.

**The six battles** (each one opens with a pixel-art "Central Dispatch" story page: the headline always reports victory, the red on the map keeps growing)

1. **The Village Well**: take a well from the "bandits" (it turns out to be dry). Tutorial; shaky enemy morale.
2. **The Paddy Road**: a machine gun covers the only dry road. Paddies are slow. So is the payroll.
3. **The Bridge**: hold a small bridge for 8 turns. HQ's promised reinforcements never come; the enemy's do.
4. **The Snow Pass**: "advance toward the rear". Get any one soldier to the flag.
5. **The Last Plane** (new): hold the airfield for 7 turns while the transport loads the gold, the archives and a piano. Seats for soldiers: on the next plane.
6. **The Last Ferry** (final battle): the enemy holds the docks with a tank they took from you, with more waves coming. HQ promises to hold the ferry. HQ is on the ferry.

**Special mechanics**

- Morale: hits, nearby deaths and leaflets lower it; at zero a soldier defects to the other side, and enemies can be turned to yours the same way.
- Paymaster: Rally restores morale around him (a one-million bonus, about one bun); Leaflets attack enemy morale.
- Grenadiers carry two grenades (cross-shaped blast), machine guns lose accuracy after moving, tanks have no morale and never surrender.
- Cover: trees, houses and sandbags lower hit chance. Three stars per battle: win, no losses, under par turns.
- Pay is shown in Gold Yuan each turn: the number goes up, the value goes down. Progress saves automatically.

**Ending**: you win all six battles and lose the war. HQ is delighted, and packing. The official report calls it a "strategic relocation", stamped TEMPORARY and VERY. The squad boards the last ferry to Taiwan from the docks it just won; on board the paymaster hands out a year of back pay in Gold Yuan, two sacks of it, which the squad offers to a seagull for one fish. The seagull declines. In Taiwan (temporary), the counterattack on the mainland is scheduled for next year, every year. Credits and a campaign tally close it out: battles won 6 of 6, wars won 0 of 1, stars redeemable on the mainland.

**Controls**

- Mouse: click a soldier → click a blue tile to move → click a marked enemy to fire (hit chance shown). Grenade, Rally, Leaflets, Hold and End Turn are in the side panel.
- Keyboard: arrows move the cursor + Enter, Tab next soldier, G grenade, R rally, L leaflets, W hold, E end turn, Esc cancel; 1–6 pick a battle on the campaign map.
- Phone: play in landscape; tap soldiers, tiles, enemies and the side-panel buttons. Top-right buttons switch language (EN / 中文), return to the map and toggle sound.
