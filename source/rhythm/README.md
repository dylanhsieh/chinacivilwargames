# 軍樂大對決 1949 Battle of the Bands 1949

類型 Genre: 節奏遊戲 Rhythm game · 語言 Languages: 繁體中文（預設）· English

Part of: [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · ▶ Play: open `index.html`

## 中文

1949年，你是國軍軍樂隊：三把小號、滿腔理想，薪水領金圓券。總部的新戰略是「軍隊打不贏天下，就讓樂隊贏回民心」。於是你帶著樂隊巡迴六個地方，每座廣場對面都站著一支共軍文工團。四條音軌（D F J K），打中音符就能留住觀眾；漏拍的話，觀眾會一個一個走到對面舞台去。你會贏下每一場樂隊大對決，然後看看這對戰爭有什麼幫助。

**1949 巡迴演出：六場演出**（每場之前都有一頁像素劇情，第一場前還有一頁「總部新戰略」序章；每場贏了之後會看到「同一時間」的戰況快報）

1. **村口廣場**（1949年1月）·〈開幕之夜波卡舞曲〉· 對手：本村秧歌隊。教學難度，腰鼓和紅綢。
2. **稻田市集**（2月）·〈稻田搖擺舞〉· 對手：土改腰鼓隊，每唱完一首就發一張地契。你只能發紀念版金圓券。
3. **江邊碼頭**（3月）·〈欠餉進行曲〉· 對手：碼頭工人宣傳合唱團。贏了之後鼓手入團（抓兵抓來的，但節奏感很好）。
4. **雪地營區**（4月）·〈冬衣春天到〉· 對手：北方前線文工團，他們有一鍋燉肉。燉肉永遠會贏。
5. **上海舞廳**（5月）·〈夜上海，金圓券〉· 對手：地下爵士小組，搖擺節奏。贏了之後低音號入團（官階比你高）。
6. **最後一班渡輪碼頭**（12月，最終決戰）·〈惡性通膨（渡輪混音版）〉· 對手：人民大聯合樂團，外加一整支軍隊當後援會。

**特殊機制**

- 觀眾向心力：左上角的藍條。打中音符（完美／很好／還行）會拉回觀眾，漏拍或長音斷掉會讓觀眾跑到對面；歸零的話「你的觀眾投共了」，椅子也一起搬走。
- 每場都有「需要」的百分比門檻（40% 到 62%），歌曲結束時觀眾必須達到門檻才算贏。
- 正面對決：歌曲最後 30%，對手樂團會往前站全力反撲，每秒都在拉走觀眾，這時候要穩住。
- 長音要按住到結束；連擊越高，分數加成越多；每 25 連擊樂隊會喊口號（「金圓券很穩定！*」）。
- 難度分簡單／普通／困難：影響音符密度、下落速度和對手反撲的力道；後面的關卡本身就更快、更密、門檻更高。
- 樂隊會成長：第三場後加入鼓手，第五場後加入低音號，觀眾回流更快。每場演出費以金圓券入帳，數字越來越大，能買的東西越來越小（一隻雞 → 一顆蛋 → …… → 什麼都買不到）。
- 自由演奏：巡演到過的曲目會解鎖，另有四首隱藏曲目在全破後解鎖。進度、最佳成績與語言自動存檔；可用 [ ] 調整音訊延遲。

**結局**：你六戰全勝，贏得樂隊大對決，群眾扛著低音號繞碼頭遊行；碼頭後方，對面樂隊的軍隊正一路往南走。同一時間長江已經被渡過，南京、上海相繼易手。政府撤退到台灣：暫時轉進，非常暫時。樂隊在甲板上演奏〈暫時，永遠〉，艦橋請他們換一首沒那麼準的。1950年，整趟巡演賺到的金圓券被櫃員直接秤重、以廢紙計價。到了台灣，反攻大陸預定明年，每一年都是明年；樂隊天天排練凱旋進行曲，從來沒有現場演出過。最後是片尾名單和巡演統計：演出勝場 6／6，打贏的戰爭 0／1，演出費 ≈ 0。

**操作**

- 鍵盤：D F J K（或 ← ↓ ↑ →）打擊四條音軌，長音請按住；選單用 W/S 選擇、A/D 調難度、Enter 開始；遊戲中 Esc 或 P 暫停（Q 放棄這首）；[ ] 調整音訊延遲。
- 滑鼠：點選場次或歌曲，再按「開演」；劇情頁點一下繼續。
- 手機：橫向遊玩，直接點四條音軌，畫面左半邊對應左兩軌、右半邊對應右兩軌（黑邊也能點），可以多指同時按。右上角可暫停、切換語言（EN／中文）和開關音效。直向時會提示把手機轉橫。

## English

1949. You are the KMT Brass Band: three horns, big dreams, paid in Gold Yuan. Headquarters' new strategy: if the army cannot win the country, the band will win the crowd. So you tour six venues, and across every square stands a Communist troupe. Four lanes (D F J K): hit the notes to keep the crowd; miss, and they wander one by one to the other stage. You will win every battle of the bands. See how much that helps the war.

**The 1949 Tour: six gigs** (each one opens with a pixel-art story page, plus a "New Strategy" prologue before the first; each win shows a "Meanwhile" news line about the real war)

1. **The Village Square** (January 1949) · "Opening Night Polka" · rival: the Village Yangge Troupe. Tutorial pace, waist drums and red silk.
2. **The Paddy Fair** (February) · "Paddy Field Shuffle" · rival: the Land Reform Waist-Drum Corps, who hand out land deeds between songs. You hand out commemorative Gold Yuan.
3. **The River Dock** (March) · "March of the Unpaid" · rival: the Dockworkers' Agitprop Choir. Winning adds a drummer (conscripted, but he has rhythm).
4. **The Snowy Camp** (April) · "The Coat Arrives in Spring" · rival: the Northern Front Song & Dance Troupe, who have stew. The stew always wins.
5. **The Shanghai Ballroom** (May) · "Shanghai Nights (Gold Yuan)" · rival: the Underground Jazz Cell, in swing time. Winning adds a tuba (it outranks you).
6. **The Last Ferry Pier** (December, final showdown) · "Hyperinflation (Ferry Mix)" · rival: the People's Massed Orchestra, with an entire army for a fan club.

**Special mechanics**

- Crowd loyalty: the blue bar, top left. Hits (Perfect / Great / OK) pull the crowd back; misses and dropped long notes send them across the square. At zero, "your audience defected" and they take the chairs.
- Every gig has a NEED threshold (40% to 62%) that the crowd must be at when the song ends.
- Showdown: in the last 30% of each song the rival troupe steps forward and pushes, draining the crowd every second. Hold your nerve.
- Hold long notes to the end; combo raises the score multiplier; every 25 combo the band shouts a slogan ("THE GOLD YUAN IS STABLE!*").
- Easy / Normal / Hard changes note density, scroll speed and how hard the rival pushes; later gigs are faster, denser and need more of the crowd on any setting.
- The band grows: a drummer after gig 3 and a tuba after gig 5 win the crowd back faster. Each gig pays a fee in Gold Yuan: the number goes up, the value goes down (one chicken → one egg → … → nothing).
- Free Play: songs unlock as the tour reaches them, plus four bonus tracks after the tour is won. Progress, best scores and language save automatically; [ ] adjusts audio offset.

**Ending**: you win all six gigs and the Battle of the Bands; the crowd carries the tuba around the pier while, behind it, the other band's army marches past, south. Meanwhile the Yangtze has been crossed and Nanjing and Shanghai have fallen. The government retreats to Taiwan: a temporary relocation, very temporary. On deck the band plays "Temporary, Forever" and the bridge asks for something less accurate. In 1950 the clerk weighs your tour earnings in Gold Yuan, as paper. In Taiwan the counterattack on the mainland is scheduled for next year, every year; the band rehearses the victory march daily, and it has never been played live. Credits and a tour tally close it out: gigs won 6 of 6, wars won 0 of 1, fees ≈ 0.

**Controls**

- Keyboard: D F J K (or ← ↓ ↑ →) hit the four lanes, hold for long notes; in menus W/S choose, A/D difficulty, Enter play; in a song Esc or P pauses (Q quits the song); [ ] adjusts audio offset.
- Mouse: click a gig or song, then PLAY; click to advance story pages.
- Phone: play in landscape and tap the four lanes; the left half of the screen covers the left two lanes and the right half the right two (the letterbox counts too), multi-touch works. Top-right buttons pause, switch language (EN / 中文) and toggle sound. Portrait shows a "turn your phone" hint.
