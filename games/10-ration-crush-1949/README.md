# 伙夫消消樂 Ration Crush 1949

**Genre / 類型:** Match-3 三消 · **Languages / 語言:** 繁體中文 (default 預設) · English
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md) · **▶ Play:** open `index.html`

## 中文

你是一支撤退中國軍部隊的伙夫，背著一口鍋，從村莊一路煮到最後一艘船。六章、十八關，每章兩餐加一位難搞的將軍：

- 收稅將軍：每四步收走你的配給
- 善變將軍：每三步想吃的東西就變
- 砲火下的將軍：倒數引信的砲彈
- 冰凍將軍：方塊會結冰
- 上海「永遠的守城將軍」：方塊一直變成金圓券
- 最後的艦長：把「那口鍋」送到最底下三次

**結局：** 伙夫打包好鍋子上船。在台北開了一間「暫時」的廚房，菜單也是暫時的；金圓券拿來墊桌腳。反攻大陸：明年！

**操作：** 交換相鄰兩格，連成三個以上。拖曳，或先點一格再點另一格。四連做出海報，L/T 形做出手榴彈，五連是「土地改革」。

## English

You are the cook of a retreating KMT unit, carrying one wok from the village to the last ship: 6 chapters, 18 levels, each chapter ending with a general who has a special rule (taxes, cravings, shells, ice, Gold Yuan tiles, and the final admiral who wants THE WOK delivered).

**Ending:** the cook packs the wok and boards the boat. In Taipei he opens a "temporary" kitchen with a temporary menu; a Gold Yuan note props up the table leg. Counterattack: next year!

**Controls:** swap neighbouring items to line up three or more (drag, or click one then the other). Four makes a Poster, L/T a Grenade, five makes LAND REFORM.
