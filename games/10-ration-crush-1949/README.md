# Ration Crush 1949

**Genre:** Match-3 puzzle  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Ten weeks of cooking for whichever army occupies the village. Posters, grenades and Land Reform specials.

> You are the village cook. Every week a different army occupies the village and demands rations. Feed them before they get creative.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- Swap two neighbouring items to line up three or more. Drag, or click one then the other.
- Four in a row makes a Poster (clears a line). An L or T makes a Grenade. Five makes LAND REFORM (clears every item of one kind).

## Notes

Gold Yuan tiles are worth less after every move. That is not a bug.
