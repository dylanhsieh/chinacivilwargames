# Conscript's Descent

**Genre:** Action RPG / roguelike  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Six floors, one life. Click-to-move looting through a burning village and the Shanghai sewers.

> Descend through a burning village and the Shanghai sewers. Loot everything. Your pay inflates. Your value doesn't.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- Mouse: left click to move and attack, right click for skill 1 · Keys 1–4 skills · F rice wine · I bag · W A S D also walk · P pause
- Phone: left thumb walks, ATTACK and skills auto-aim at the nearest enemy.

## Notes

Satire. Permadeath. The quartermaster accepts Gold Yuan, reluctantly.
