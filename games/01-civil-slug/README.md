# Civil Slug 1946

**Genre:** Run-and-gun  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Five side-scrolling missions in the style of classic arcade run-and-guns, with vehicles to hijack and prisoners to free.

> A run-and-gun about a war where the enemy was usually somebody's cousin.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- ← → move · ↑ aim up · ↓ crouch · J fire (knife up close) · K jump · L grenade / cannon · ↓+K leave vehicle · P pause
- Phone: thumb-stick left, buttons right. Tap FIRE to skip story scenes.

## Notes

Satire. Both armies get roasted equally. The villagers lost either way.
