# 國共大戰 Civil Slug 1946

**Genre / 類型:** Run-and-gun 橫向射擊 · **Languages / 語言:** 繁體中文 (default 預設) · English
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md)

**▶ Play:** open `index.html` in any browser (desktop or phone). One self-contained file, works offline.

## 中文

一款致敬《越南大戰》(Metal Slug) 的像素橫向射擊遊戲。你是一名國軍士兵，1946 到 1949 年，五個關卡、五個頭目，搶坦克、騎驢子、救村民、躲標語。

你會打贏每一場仗。然後呢？然後就上船了。**明年就反攻大陸！**（每年都這麼說。）

- 五個關卡：村莊、稻田、雪原、長江、上海，各有頭目戰與結算畫面
- 結局：任務 5/5 勝利，內戰 0/1 勝利，政府「暫時」撤退到台灣
- 中英雙語：標題畫面右上角或遊戲中按「中文 / EN」切換，會記住你的選擇
- 金圓券計分：分數用金圓券計，一直在貶值

**操作：** ← → 移動 · ↑ 向上瞄準 · ↓ 蹲下 · J 開火（近身用刀）· K 跳 · L 手榴彈/砲 · ↓+K 下車 · P 暫停
**手機：** 左手搖桿，右手按鈕；點擊畫面跳過劇情。建議橫向遊玩。

## English

A Metal Slug-style pixel run-and-gun. You are a Nationalist (KMT) soldier, 1946–49: five missions, five bosses, hijacked tanks, a donkey, villagers to rescue, slogans to dodge.

You will win every battle. Then you will be on a boat. *"We counterattack the mainland next year!"* (Said every year.)

- Five missions (village, paddies, snowfields, the Yangtze, Shanghai) with boss fights and tally screens
- Ending: missions won 5/5, civil wars won 0/1; the government "temporarily" relocates to Taiwan
- Bilingual: toggle 中文 / EN at the top right of the title screen or in-game; your choice is remembered
- Score in Gold Yuan, which keeps losing value

**Controls:** ← → move · ↑ aim up · ↓ crouch · J fire (knife up close) · K jump · L grenade / cannon · ↓+K leave vehicle · P pause
**Phone:** thumb-stick left, buttons right; tap to skip story scenes. Landscape recommended.

*Satire. Both armies get roasted. The villagers lost either way.*
