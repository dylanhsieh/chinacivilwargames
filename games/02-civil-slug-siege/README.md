# Civil Slug: Village Siege

**Genre:** First-person shooter (3D)  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Defend, and then take, one village in first person. A tank with six owners, a conscript paid in Gold Yuan.

> One village. One tank with six owners. One conscript, paid in Gold Yuan.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- W A S D move · mouse to aim (click the game to capture it) · click / J fire, knife up close · Space jump · G grenade · Q swap · ← → ↑ ↓ also aim · P pause
- Phone: left thumb walks, right thumb drags to aim in any direction.

## Notes

Satire. Nobody in this war comes out looking good. Especially the villagers' landlord.
