# Ming An: Expedition 48

**Genre:** Turn-based RPG with real-time dodging  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Explore Shanghai in 3D and fight turn-based battles with timed dodges and parries, in the spirit of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33.

> Shanghai, 1948. Every year the Printer paints a bigger number on the banknote, and everyone whose savings are worth less than it is erased. Expedition 48 sets out to stop it. Expedition 47 left a note: bring more money.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- Explore: W A S D · mouse look (click the game) · Shift run · E use · J or click to strike first
- Battle: W S choose · J / Enter confirm · K back · press J at the gold ring for skill bonus
- Enemy turn: SPACE dodge as the blow lands · SHIFT parry (tighter) · parry every hit to counter

## Notes

Satire. Turn-based with real-time dodging. Both sides get roasted, and so does the central bank.
