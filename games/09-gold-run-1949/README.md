# Gold Run 1949

**Genre:** Tower defense  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Guard the gold reserves on the Shanghai docks until the last boat sails. Tower prices rise every wave.

> Shanghai, 1949. The gold reserves are leaving for Taiwan by ship. Everyone in the city would like a souvenir. Guard the crates until the last boat sails.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- Click or tap a tower in the bar, then an empty tile to build it. Click a tower to upgrade or sell it.
- 1–5 choose a tower · Space next wave · F fast forward · Esc cancel

## Notes

Satire. Tower prices rise every wave. Your printing presses make it worse. That is the joke, and also the history.
