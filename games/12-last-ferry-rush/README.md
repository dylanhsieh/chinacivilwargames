# Last Ferry Rush

**Genre:** Racing  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

A pseudo-3D arcade racer: drive a truck of passengers from the village to the docks before the last ferry leaves.

> A requisitioned truck, a full load of passengers, and the last ferry leaves at dawn. Pass the checkpoints for more time. Hit things and passengers fall off.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- ↑ / W accelerate · ↓ / S brake · ← → / A D steer
- Phone: the truck accelerates on its own. Use the arrows to steer and BRAKE to slow down.

## Notes

Fares are paid in Gold Yuan. The meter is accurate. The money is not.
