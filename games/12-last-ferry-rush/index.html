LAST FERRY RUSH1949 · RACING · SIX LEGS TO THE LAST FERRY

1948–49. You drive a requisitioned Nationalist army truck full of passengers to the last evacuation ferry. The government is relocating. Temporarily, it says. Six legs, from a burning village to the docks; the Communists hold the roadblocks, the bridges and, increasingly, the map.

Satire. Checkpoints add time, crashes cost passengers, hitchhikers waving at the roadside climb aboard. Fares are paid in Gold Yuan: the meter is accurate, the money is not. Progress saves automatically.
