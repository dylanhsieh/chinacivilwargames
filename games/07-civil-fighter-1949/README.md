# Civil Fighter 1949

**Genre:** Fighting game  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Four fighters, best of three rounds, specials and supers. Arcade mode or two players on one keyboard.

> Four fighters. Two armies. One printing press. Settle the civil war the only fair way: best of three rounds.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- Player 1: A D walk · W jump · S crouch · hold back to block · J light · K heavy · L special · I super
- Special + direction: neutral = projectile · forward = rush · down = anti-air · back = counter. Classic motions work too (↓↘→ + attack, →↓↘ + attack).
- Player 2: arrow keys · 1 2 3 0 (numpad) or , . / ;
- Phone: left thumb moves, buttons on the right.

## Notes

Satire. Nobody wins a civil war. Some people win rounds.
