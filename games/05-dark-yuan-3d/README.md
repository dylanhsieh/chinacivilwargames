# Dark Yuan 3D

**Genre:** Souls-like (3D)  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

The 3D version of Dark Yuan, in blocky low-poly 3D: lock-on, rolls, parries and bosses on the way to the last ferry.

> China, 1948. Soldiers who die without discharge papers get back up and keep fighting. You died in a roped column of conscripts. Nobody discharged you. Reach the last ferry.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- W A S D move · mouse look (click the game to capture the mouse) · left click / J attack · U heavy · right click / L guard (tap to parry) · Space roll (hold to sprint) · Q / middle click lock on · R rice wine · G grenade · E use · arrow keys turn camera · P pause
- Phone: left thumb moves, drag the right side to turn the camera, buttons for the rest

## Notes

Satire. Very hard. Your Gold Yuan loses value every time you rest. When you die, it keeps losing value on the ground.
