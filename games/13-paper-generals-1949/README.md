# Paper Generals 1949

**Genre:** Turn-based tactics  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Five grid battles for either army. Morale is a resource, and soldiers whose morale runs out switch sides.

> Command a squad for either army. Both sides pay in paper: one in Gold Yuan, one in leaflets. Soldiers whose morale runs out change sides, and so can yours.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- Click a soldier, click a blue tile to move, click a marked enemy to fire. Grenades, Rally and Leaflets are in the side panel.
- arrows + Enter, Tab next soldier, G R L W for abilities, E end turn, Esc cancel.

## Notes

Morale is a resource. So, technically, is the enemy.
