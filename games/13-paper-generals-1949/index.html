PAPER GENERALS1949 · TURN-BASED TACTICS · FIVE BATTLES

Command a squad for either army. Both sides pay in paper: one in Gold Yuan, one in leaflets. Soldiers whose morale runs out change sides, and so can yours.

Click a soldier, click a blue tile to move, click a marked enemy to fire. Grenades, Rally and Leaflets are in the side panel.
Keys: arrows + Enter, Tab next soldier, G R L W for abilities, E end turn, Esc cancel.

Morale is a resource. So, technically, is the enemy.
