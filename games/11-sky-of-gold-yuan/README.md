# Sky of Gold Yuan

**Genre:** Shoot 'em up  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

A vertical shooter in a borrowed biplane. Three stages, power-ups, money bombs and the Printer's airship.

> A borrowed biplane, a mercenary contract paid by the hour, and a sky full of people who also have not been paid. Fly to Shanghai and shoot down the Printer's airship.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- W A S D or arrows fly · J or Space fire (hold) · K money bomb
- Phone: drag anywhere to fly (it fires on its own) · button for money bomb

## Notes

Collect P for more guns, B for bombs, ¥ for score. Your score is in Gold Yuan, so it is mostly decorative.
