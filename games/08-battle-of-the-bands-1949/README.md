# Battle of the Bands 1949

**Genre:** Rhythm game  
**Part of:** [China Civil War Games](../../README.md), a series set in China, 1948–49

Two propaganda troupes, one village square, four lanes. Hit the notes to keep the crowd.

> The village has one square and two propaganda troupes. Hit the notes, keep the crowd. Miss, and they wander over to the other stage. Nobody asked the village.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, desktop or phone. It is a single self-contained file: no install, no build step, works offline (the pixel fonts load from Google Fonts when online and fall back otherwise). Turn the sound on; all music and effects are synthesized live.

## Controls

- D F J K (or ← ↓ ↑ →) · hold for long notes · W/S or ↑↓ pick songs · A/D difficulty · Enter play · Esc back · [ ] audio offset
- Phone: tap the four lanes.

## Notes

Turn the sound on. This one is mostly sound.
